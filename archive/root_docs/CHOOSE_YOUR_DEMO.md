## What's Different?

### Image Pattern

**Demo 1 - Gaussian Blob:**
```
A symmetric blob:
      # # #
    # # @ # #
      # # #

Same pattern in every frame
MRI-like: resembles brain cross-section
```

**Demo 2 - Sine Wave:**
```
A traveling wave:
  ~ . . . . .      . ~ . . . .      . . ~ . . .
  ~ ~ . . . .  vs  . ~ ~ . . .  vs  . . ~ ~ . .
  ~ ~ @ . . .      . ~ @ ~ . .      . . ~ @ ~ .

Pattern changes each frame
Animated: wave travels across the volume
```

---

## Data Format Difference

**Demo 1:**
- Raw float32 binary data
- Generated by embedded Python
- Simple binary stream
- Size: 1.47 MB per frame

**Demo 2:**
- ISMRMRD Image format (header + data)
- Generated by image_streamer C++ client
- Industry-standard MRI format
- Size: ~1.50 MB per frame (includes header)

---

## Performance View

Both demos show these metrics:
```
Marshal latency:     ~3.6ms
SWMR flush:          ~50ms
Visualizer latency:  ~5ms
Total latency:       ~58ms
Robot throughput:    ~280 ops/sec
```

**Difference:**
- Demo 1: 5 frames → 5 visualizer updates (every 1.5s)
- Demo 2: 20 frames → 20 visualizer updates (every 50-100ms)

**Demo 2 is better at showing** the 20ms HTTP polling interval works.
